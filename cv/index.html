Publications

  • 2026
    Peer-Aware Distributed Path Planning for a Production-Deployed Multi-AGV Fleet in a Human-Occupied Garment Factory
    Manuscript in preparation (2026) — First author

    Decentralized A* path-planning and coordination scheme deployed on a 3-robot AGV fleet running inside a live garment factory alongside hundreds of workers with no physical separation. Each robot publishes its plan as timestamped grid waypoints; peers treat them as spatiotemporal reservations with three conflict checks (node / edge-swap / final-cell). Continuous timestamp maintenance keeps ETAs honest between replans, and obstacles are handled without dropping the active plan. Months of zero-collision production operation; case study reports protocol + throughput / recovery measurements. Corresponding author; manuscript in preparation.

  • 2026
    Experimental Speed Synchronization of a Dual-BLDC Tracked Differential-Drive Vehicle Using a GA-Tuned Bilateral Cross-Coupled Controller on a Dual-MCU Architecture
    Manuscript under review (2026) — First author

    Distributed dual-MCU implementation of bilateral cross-coupled speed synchronization for a tracked differential-drive vehicle driven by two deliberately mismatched 3-phase BLDC motors. Each motor regulated by its own MCU running a PID loop with velocity/acceleration feedforward; a bilateral cross-coupled term, evaluated identically on both nodes from a dedicated 1 ms inter-MCU speed exchange, drives the inter-motor speed difference toward zero. All gains GA-tuned offline. Reduces peak transient inter-motor speed error by 79 %, RMS by 73 %, accumulated straight-line heading drift by 80 % vs an independent-PID baseline. Corresponding author; draft linked above.
