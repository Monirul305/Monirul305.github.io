A complete **electrical-services design package** for a seven-storey residential building — the standard end-to-end design exercise in BUET EEE's building services / utilities course.

**Scope of the design.**

- **Load estimation** — per-room lighting, general-purpose outlets, fixed appliances (air-conditioners, water pumps, lift), with applied diversity factors to size each panel and the main intake.
- **Panel scheduling** — sub-distribution panels per floor with circuit allocations, breaker ratings, and balanced phase loading across the 3-phase incoming supply.
- **Conductor sizing** — cable cross-sections selected against current-carrying capacity, voltage drop, and short-circuit withstand, per the applicable national wiring code.
- **Lighting and power layouts** — floor-plan drawings showing fixture positions, switching arrangements, outlet locations, and final-circuit grouping.
- **Riser diagram** — vertical schematic showing the main intake, meter board, busbar / cable risers, sub-distribution panels per floor, and the connections between them.
- **Earthing scheme** — earth electrode design, earth-continuity conductors, and equipotential bonding for the building.

The output is the full set of drawings and schedules an electrical contractor would actually use to wire the building — not just a circuit analysis exercise. The work is grounded in **standard building-services practice** rather than novel circuit design, but it covers the full design / code-compliance / drawing pipeline that any practicing power engineer in Bangladesh has to handle.
