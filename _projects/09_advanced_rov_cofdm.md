Next-generation ROV demonstrating expanded capability: a 6-DOF robotic arm
carrying **20 kg while moving at 5 km/h**, paired with a comprehensive
multi-camera and wireless video system.

### Vision System

Six IP cameras: Main PTZ · Claw PTZ · 3× cameras with 4× optical zoom ·
All with night-vision capability · Thermal camera for heat-signature monitoring.

Live video transmitted via **1.4 GHz COFDM** at up to **1 km range** with
low-latency, secure video.

### Control

- **HCU (Handheld Control Unit)** — short-range operation
- **OCU (Operation Control Unit)** — long-range
- **Wired optical fallback** — for RF-contested environments

### Tech Stack

`1.4 GHz COFDM` · `IP cameras` · `PTZ control` · `Thermal imaging` · `Night vision` · `STM32`
