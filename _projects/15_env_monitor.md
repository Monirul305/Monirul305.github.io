Developed a self-sustaining environmental monitoring node using Arduino, solar
panels, and a GSM module to continuously collect and transmit temperature,
humidity, and CO₂ concentration data over the cellular network. PCB designed
in Autodesk Eagle.

### Tech Stack
`Arduino` · `GSM` · `Solar power` · `Autodesk Eagle` · `IoT sensors`
