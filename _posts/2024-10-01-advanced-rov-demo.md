We're thrilled to share a demonstration of our newest **ROV in action** — showcasing the capabilities and versatility we built into this platform at [Cybernetics Hi-Tech Solutions](https://cyberneticsbd.com).

### 🦾 Precision & Power

- Equipped with a **6-DOF manipulator**, the ROV delivers exceptional accuracy in pick-and-place operations — even while carrying heavy loads up to **20 kg at speeds up to 5 km/h**.

### 🎥 Advanced Camera System

- **Six IP cameras** transmit live video feeds via a **1.4 GHz COFDM transmitter**, ensuring low-latency, reliable, and secure video transmission across a **1 km range**.
- The **Main PTZ** and **Claw PTZ** cameras offer a full view of the surroundings; the other three feature **4× optical zoom** for enhanced detail. All of them have **night-vision** capability.
- A dedicated **thermal camera** handles heat-signature monitoring in the surroundings.

### 📡 Dual Control Systems

- The ROV can be operated through a **Handheld Control Unit (HCU)** for short-range use, or an **Operation Control Unit (OCU)** for long-range communications.
- For environments with heavy network interference, we've also included a **wired optical fallback** — fibre tether to maintain flawless operation when the wireless channels degrade.

This ROV is designed for performance in demanding environments, offering reliability, precision, and versatility together in one platform. Check out the demo and see the future of remote manipulation in action!
