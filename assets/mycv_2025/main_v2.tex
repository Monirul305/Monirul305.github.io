\section{\textbf{Research Experience}}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=3.5pt, parsep=0pt, topsep=3pt]

    \item \textbf{Distributed multi-AGV path coordination --- deployed in production.} Coordination scheme for a 3-robot fleet with \textbf{months of zero-collision operation} along a 68\,m factory corridor shared with several hundred workers and no physical barrier: each robot plans its own A* path and publishes timestamped grid waypoints that peers treat as spatiotemporal reservations, while a fail-closed coordinator (FleetCore) handles job dispatch, mutual exclusion and deadlock recovery. \vspace{-5pt}

    \item \textbf{GA-tuned multi-rate cross-coupled BLDC speed synchronization.} Two mismatched BLDC motors, one MCU each; a bilateral cross-coupled term on the inter-motor difference runs at 1\,kHz over 200\,Hz per-motor loops. Twelve gains GA-tuned \emph{on the assembled 120\,kg vehicle}. Against an equally tuned independent-drive baseline: clean-tracking RMSE $-$41\% (3.9$\sigma$), one-sided-load error $-$65\%; a same-plant comparison shows the multi-rate loop, not the coupling law, is the lever. \vspace{-5pt}

    \item \textbf{Latency-compensated 5-state EKF for visual-fiducial localization.} Fuses wheel-encoder odometry with AprilTag fixes; velocity-bias states absorb systematic encoder drift over long corridors, and a rewind-and-replay step applies each camera fix at its true measurement timestamp. \vspace{-5pt}

    \item \textbf{Closed-form analytical IK for a tracked mobile manipulator.} From-scratch analytical solver replacing MoveIt's KDL with deterministic, branch-stable solutions; smooth turret-scaling preserves continuity through the home-pose singularity and an FK-anchored accumulator suppresses un-commanded cross-axis motion. \vspace{-5pt}

    \item \textbf{Dual-network feedback for a tele-operated multi-node vehicle.} One bare-metal STM32 masters an RS-485 bus over eleven nodes while terminating a W5500 TCP/IP uplink. A discrete-event model of the firmware's scheduling identifies the uplink \emph{reply format}, not the bus, as dominating telemetry age; a full-state reply cuts 99th-percentile age from 2.1\,s to 273\,ms. \vspace{-5pt}

    \item \textbf{Battery-sizing optimizer for a high-payload multirotor.} Design and aerodynamic simulation of a heavy-lift multirotor, plus an in-house optimizer that computes feasible battery-pack configurations from payload, flight-time and motor parameters. Published as an interactive client-side tool. \vspace{-5pt}

\end{itemize}
