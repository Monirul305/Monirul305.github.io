\section{\textbf{Research Contributions \& Field Deployments}}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]

    \item \textbf{Production Multi-AGV Fleet (live garment factory).} Lead engineer end-to-end for a 3-robot AGV fleet operating continuously alongside hundreds of human workers with no physical separation. Designed FleetCore (ROS 2 dispatcher with per-robot FSMs, mutual exclusion at shared resources, layered timeouts, fail-closed substrate), a custom 5-state EKF with camera-latency compensation for AprilTag-based localization, and a fully decentralized A* path-planner with peer-aware timestamped reservations (three conflict checks: node, edge-swap, final-cell). Months of zero-collision production operation. \textit{Productised as Cybernetics CyberFleet.} \vspace{-5pt}

    \item \textbf{7-DOF Mobile Manipulator (ROS 2 / MoveIt 2).} Owned the full control / software stack. Replaced MoveIt's KDL solver with a custom closed-form analytical IK; added smooth turret scaling for the home-pose singularity and an FK-anchored goal accumulator that cut un-commanded cross-axis motion ~5\(\times\). ~3,500 lines of Python across coordinator, IK, FK, SBus decoder, and a 40-test unit suite; full URDF/SRDF + ros2\_control integration. \textit{Cybernetics ``Jontro Soinik 2.0''.} \vspace{-5pt}

    \item \textbf{Indigenous EOD ROV Family (Jontro Soinik v1 \& v2).} v1 (Mali UN deployment, 2024) and v2 (Congo UN deployment, 2025). Designed v1's star-topology communication network and its S-curve arm-motion profile; for v2, designed the custom multi-drop bus and the differential-drive speed-synchronization layer (subject of a first-author manuscript). All controller PCBs designed in Altium / KiCad and milled in-house on a 3-axis CNC. \textit{Cybernetics.} \vspace{-5pt}

    \item \textbf{Heavy-Lift Multirotor (50 kg payload).} Designed BMS, propulsion stack, frame, and power-distribution PCBs for a heavy-lift cargo / emergency-response drone. The battery + motor sizing study has been ported into a browser-side Drone Battery Optimizer (Pareto search over Y\textsubscript{s}~\(\times\)~Z\textsubscript{p} pack configurations) published on the portfolio site. \textit{Cybernetics.} \vspace{-5pt}

    \item \textbf{Vision-Guided Target-Tracking Drone (SD15-N).} Designed the autonomous target-tracking pipeline: YOLOv11n on a Raspberry Pi 5 closed-loop with a Pixhawk 2.4.8 over MAVLink. \textit{Cybernetics.} \vspace{-5pt}

    \item \textbf{Industrial CoreXY 3D Printer (Titan 550).} Two-iteration product build: 510~\(\times\)~510~\(\times\)~550~mm volume, \(\pm 0.02\)~mm repeatability, BigTreeTech + TMC2209, Marlin 2.0; in-house workhorse for every other platform's fixtures. \textit{Cybernetics.} \vspace{-5pt}

    \item \textbf{\(\alpha\)-Graphyne Ballistic Nanoribbon FET.} BUET undergraduate research with Dr. Mahbub Alam — modelled ballistic quantum transport in \(\alpha\)-graphyne nanoribbons; published in \textit{Micromachines} (MDPI), 2023.

\end{itemize}
