Production-Deployed 3-Robot AGV Fleet (ROS 2, AprilTag EKF, Distributed Path Planning)

Production-deployed autonomous guided vehicle fleet navigating a live garment factory alongside hundreds of workers. ROS 2, AprilTag EKF, distributed path planning, FleetCore coordinator.

Indigenous EOD ROV Family — UN Peacekeeping Deployment (Mali, Congo)

Vision-Guided Target-Tracking Surveillance Drone (YOLOv11n on Raspberry Pi 5 + Pixhawk)

undergraduate

6-DOF Robotic Arm — Stepper-Driven with 3D-Printed Planetary Gearboxes

Six-degree-of-freedom serial robotic arm — SolidWorks frame, body fabricated on a hobby 3D printer I built in parallel, stepper-motor actuation through custom 3D-printed planetary gearboxes, Arduino-based control, and analytical inverse kinematics with closed-form algebraic solutions.

Hobby 3D Printer — Designed from Scratch in SolidWorks, Laser-Cut Acrylic, Custom Marlin

A 3D printer designed from scratch as a personal hobby build during COVID-19 — SolidWorks for every component, laser-cut acrylic frame, Arduino Mega controller, 5x NEMA 17 stepper motors, and a customised Marlin firmware. Distinct from the later Titan 550 industrial product.

Vehicle Detection & Speed Estimation from Traffic Footage (YOLOv2 + MATLAB)

Trained a YOLOv2 detector on a self-curated vehicle dataset and built a frame-by-frame tracking layer to estimate real-world vehicle speeds from monocular traffic video. BUET EEE coursework.

RV32I Multicycle Processor with Wishbone Memory Controller (Verilog · FPGA · TSMC 180 nm)

From-scratch RV32I multicycle processor in Verilog with a custom Wishbone-interfaced memory controller, full testbench-based verification, FPGA validation, and a TSMC 180 nm physical-design flow in Cadence. Supervised by Dr. A. B. M. Harun-Ur-Rashid (BUET EEE).

Autonomous GPS-Waypoint Delivery Drone (SolidWorks · Arduino · IMU + GPS · PID)

Quadcopter built end-to-end for autonomous waypoint-based parcel delivery — SolidWorks airframe, Arduino flight controller with IMU attitude stabilisation, GPS waypoint navigation, and a servo-actuated payload release. BUET EEE undergraduate build.

Solar-Powered Remote Environmental Monitor (Arduino · GSM · ThingSpeak · Relay-Switched Battery Bank)

Self-sustaining outdoor environmental node — Arduino UNO + GSM cellular uplink to ThingSpeak, powered by a solar-charged battery bank with a relay-switched series-on-discharge / parallel-on-charge topology to feed the GSM modem's high transient current. EEE 416 group project at BUET.

Regulated DC-DC Boost Converter — Schematic & Layout in TSMC 180 nm (Cadence)

Designed and laid out a regulated DC-DC boost converter on TSMC 180 nm in Cadence — schematic capture, transistor-level simulation, and physical layout with parasitic extraction. VLSI coursework at BUET EEE.

Closed-Loop Induction-Motor Speed Control — V/f + SVPWM in MATLAB Simulink

3-phase induction motor closed-loop speed control via V/f (volts-per-hertz) scalar control with SVPWM (Space Vector PWM) inverter modulation. Full Simulink model with speed reference, flux/voltage scheduling, and PI compensation. BUET EEE coursework.

Optical Particle-Concentration Detector — Laser Light-Scattering with Webcam Readout

Low-cost airborne-particle concentration sensor using laser illumination and a webcam-based intensity readout — measures the light scattered off particle–photon interaction. Built as a cost-conscious alternative to commercial particle counters. BUET EEE coursework.

Laboratory Variable DC Power Supply — Linear-Regulator Bench Build

Bench-grade variable DC power supply — linear-regulator architecture with adjustable output voltage, current limiting, and short-circuit protection. Built end-to-end including transformer secondary, rectification, filtering, regulation, and enclosure. BUET EEE coursework.

Optical Mark Recognition (OMR) Machine — Built Entirely from Discrete Digital Logic

Built an OMR (Optical Mark Recognition) machine for multiple-choice answer sheets using only discrete digital logic ICs — no microcontroller. Optical sensing, counters, and decoders implement the full mark-detection and tally pipeline. BUET EEE coursework.

Seven-Storey Building Electrical Services Design

Complete electrical-services design for a seven-storey residential building — load estimation, panel scheduling, conductor sizing, lighting and power layouts, and earthing scheme. BUET EEE coursework on building services engineering.
